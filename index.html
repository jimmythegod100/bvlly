Why generic pet clothes miss

Miniature exotic bulldogs are compact and thick through the chest. Most “small dog” garments are graded from longer, narrower bodies.

Chest-first

We grade from girth, not from back length. The cloth has somewhere to sit.

Short saddle

Hem hits near the last rib so it does not tent over the loin or cover the tail set.

Walk-ready

Necks and withers leave room for a harness. Hardware is sized for a thick neck, not a toy breed.
